Sprint rules — short, because the chain does the enforcing

  • The window is UTC Monday 00:00 → next Monday 00:00. One shared clock, no timezone games — fill timestamps are hash-committed.
  • A round counts only if it opened and closed inside the window. A bag carried in was last week's decision; a bag carried out has no honest realized number yet.
  • Score is the same formula as the main board — window ROI × ln(1+rounds) × discipline — applied to the week's slice only.
  • No separate entry, no reset. Your normal practice IS your entry. The sprint is a lens on the same committed record, which is why it can't be gamed separately.
