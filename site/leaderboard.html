How ranking stays honest

  • Replayed, not trusted. Standings are computed from your raw fills server-side. The claimed P&L is only compared against the replay — a mismatch is a signal, never a rank.
  • Re-priced against history. Every fill is checked against the token's real traded range in that exact minute. Fabricated prices reject the record.
  • Extend-only. A submitted chain must extend the one you already committed. Deleting a bad week and starting fresh breaks the anchor.
  • Score = ROI × ln(1+rounds) × discipline. Return on declared bankroll, weighted by sustained rounds, discounted for revenge re-entries and drawdown. Five closed rounds minimum. One moonshot doesn't top this board.
  • One record per X account. Sybils cost a real, public identity each.
